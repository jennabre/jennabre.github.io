Game: Mitzi's Birthday

It's Mitzi the monkey's birthday, but dinner got burned! Luckily, the fridge is stocked with Mitzi's favorite fruits. Her friends suggested a game: they will blindfold her and drop fruit for her to catch. Unfortunately, a prankster friend is dropping Mitzi's least favorite food, onions.

Goal

Help Mitzi catch the fruit (🍌🥭🍎🍏🍊) and avoid the onions (🧅) for a tasty birthday meal.

How it works

  • Move Mitzi side-to-side with arrow keys or WASD.
  • To win and get birthday cake, eat all the food required.
  • Don't let Mitzi's mood drop too low or she'll give up.
Food tips:
  • Apples (🍎🍏) are high in fiber. They fill Mitzi up faster.
  • Onions (🧅) are gross. She won't eat them. Tasting them makes her sadder.
  • Bananas (🍌) are Mitzi's favorite. They improve her mood.
